MJOLNIR

A cinematic lightning cursor. Your pointer becomes an enchanted war-hammer that leaves branching electricity in its wake and cracks the page open when you click.

Integration guide

Move to trail sparks · click to slam · hold and drag for a storm

01 — Motion

It reacts to how you move

The hammer eases toward the pointer, tilts into its direction of travel, and bobs gently when you leave it alone. Velocity drives the tilt, the glow and the amount of electrical activity — so slow drifting stays subtle and fast sweeps throw sparks.

Slow

Drift across this panel. Barely a whisper of static.

Fast

Now whip the pointer back and forth. Sparks shed off the head.

Idle

Stop moving. The hammer floats and pulses on its own.

02 — Impact

Every click lands like thunder

A click swings the hammer down, detonates a white-hot flash, fires branching bolts outward, kicks up sparks and pushes a shockwave ring — then recoils. The whole event resolves in well under a second so it never gets in your way.

Click anywhere in this arena
03 — Trails

Hold the button and paint with lightning

While dragging, the cursor emits a continuous chain of independently jittered arcs — branching, flickering, and short-lived, so it reads as electricity rather than a smooth neon line. Nothing is ever left behind on the page.

05 — Never in the way

The page still works, completely

The overlay is pointer-events: none, so nothing below it is ever blocked. Text stays selectable, inputs keep their caret, scrollbars and dropdowns behave. Try everything here.

06 — Integration

Drop it into anything

No build step required, and no framework assumed.

Plain HTML

<script src="thor-cursor.min.js"></script>
<script>
  ThorCursor.init();
</script>

ES modules

import { ThorCursor } from 'thor-cursor';

ThorCursor.init({ intensity: 1.2 });

React / Next.js

useEffect(() => {
  const c = ThorCursor.create();
  return () => c.destroy();
}, []);
